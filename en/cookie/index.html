What stays on the device

The theme choice is a local-storage entry, theme. Closing the notice at the bottom is another entry, cta_popup_closed. They are not profiling cookies and these pages do not send them to a server of their own.

The root page looks at the browser language and opens Italian or English. It does not save a preference.

What the pages load

The typeface comes from Google Fonts. The style comes from a Tailwind CDN. The icons come from a CDN. Those are requests to third parties. This site does not add an analytics tag on top of those requests.

GitHub Pages hosts the files. The host logs belong to GitHub, under GitHub's terms, not to a form on these pages.

What these pages do not do

There is no form. No email address is collected. No account is opened. The engine, if you start it from the repository, is another program: its memory does not pass through this page.
