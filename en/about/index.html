What is published

The engine, the page, the tests, and the bilingual manual are in the repository. The site you are reading is the presentation. It does not rebuild the manual and it is not the place where a connection is configured.

The trial starts with an empty Espo. Production dumps are not imported. The Advanced Pack is not in the repository.

What it is not

It is not a certified connector. The recipes are a mapping to review. There is no service price list, there is no contact form, there is no company to cite on this page.

The Gruppo Meridia demo, in the manual, is the proof of a run. It is not a second product and it is not a customer published here.

Where the rest is read

What is done and what is not done live in the first chapter of the manual, in the repository. If a sentence on this site and a sentence in the manual diverge, the manual wins: it is the text that changes together with the code.
