What they propose

Accounts, contacts, sites, and the price list are four starting points. Each names a sheet and some columns. They do not write on their own and they do not certify that the system on the other side has a particular name: that name is not in the code.

Propose the mapping uses synonyms, in the language of the page, and does not save until a person confirms. An unrecognized column stays empty.

What they do not promise

They are not a certified connector. They do not replace the ERP. They do not create Espo users. They do not turn write-back on.

The price list points at Product. If this installation does not have the sheet, the fields stay empty. The recipe does not invent the catalog.

Before turning it on

Look at the preview. Decide the owner of the fields a person corrects by hand. Leave write-back off until the return columns are named.

The switch-on plan is this: connection, sheet, mapping reread, run. The handover page does not contain passwords.
