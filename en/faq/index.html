Beyond the list

Nesso is not a second CRM. It always has Espo on one side and a channel on the other. Writes toward Espo go through the HTTP API. There is no query on the tables.

The retry queue, the portal filter, the seal, and write-back exist and stay off. The trial does not turn them on. Without NESSO_SECRET_KEY the password in the volume stays cleartext.

The recipes are not a certified connector. The price list looks at Product: if the sheet is missing, the fields stay empty. Mail stays in Espo.
