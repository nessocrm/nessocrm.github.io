The ones Espo already has

The engine does not invent entity names. It reads the sheets this installation exposes. On a standard sheet the external key is the custom field cExternalKey.

Two integrations on the same sheet do not step on each other: the key contains the integration id. An accounts file and a sites file can both land on Account, with different keys.

The ones created here

If the sheet does not exist, Nesso creates it through the API. Singular and plural are given. Espo adds the prefix C. Fields on the new sheet do not receive a second prefix. The key is called externalKey.

A sheet created here is not a second CRM. It is an Espo sheet, visible to whoever has the team. The team and the assigned person come from a column or from a fixed team.

The ones that are not touched

Espo users are not created from a master. A column of people does not become a list of logins.

The recipe price list points at Product. If this installation does not have the sheet, the price-list fields stay empty and the recipe does not invent the catalog.
