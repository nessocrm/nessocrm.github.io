Who has to trust the run

Four fears, four answers already in the engine

They are not characters. They are the people who, in a company with Espo and an ERP, have to say yes. The site does not promise they will switch it on alone on Monday.

Owner

Wants one number: today's accounts are in sync, and the balance of the totals if there is an amount. It is a check. It does not write.

Sales

Wrote a note yesterday. The field belongs to Espo: tonight's file does not clear it. The sheet shows who wrote last.

Finance

A bad file must not close customers in silence. There is the preview, a VAT number already present, and undoing the run.

Installer

Four steps, a recipe to review, a switch-on plan, a handover page without passwords. Then they can leave.

The field owner

The source wins. Even if the cell is empty.

That is the default, and it is on purpose. A field can have another owner: Espo. Then the run does not touch it. Without that distinction, "sync" means deleting a person's work.

  • The key is the integration plus the external value. Two systems do not trample each other's records.
  • A row that disappears from the file is closed in Espo and stays in the history. It is not deleted.
  • Writing back to the ERP stays off until you turn it on.

Two objects

The connection carries. The integration decides.

01

Connection

The physical channel. Webhook, database, file, or outbound HTTP. One connection can have several integrations.

02

Sheet

Account, Contact, Lead, or a sheet Espo already has in the metadata. If it is missing, you create it: the technical name takes the prefix C.

03

Mapping

Which column lands in which field, and who owns it. The synonym proposal does not save. The model, without an address, does not start.

04

Run

By hand, every 5 minutes, every 15, every hour, or at a fixed time. The same integration does not start twice.
